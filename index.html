Welcome to Adi's Dentalaya

Quality dental care, close to home

Adi's Dentalaya is a multispeciality dental and oral clinic located in the 2nd block of Rajajinagar, on Ram Mandir Road. The clinic is placed for easy access, so arriving on time for your care is one less thing to worry about.

Our services range from dental diagnosis, to fillings, root canal treatment, extractions, to crowns/cap and bridge work. We also provide dental implants, smile designing, orthodontic corrections, periodontal surgeries and advanced dental treatment procedures.

Cosmetic dentistry

A brighter smile, restrained by what is right for you

Professional teeth whitening and bleaching can brighten your smile in a single visit, and porcelain veneers can change the position, shape, size and colour of teeth. Cosmetic dental bonding is a quick, affordable option for chipped or discoloured teeth.

We discuss cosmetic options honestly — including who is a good candidate and what each treatment can and cannot do — so you can decide with clear expectations.

Our approach

What a visit typically looks like

No unclear proposals and no rushed decisions — the clinic's care pathway is built around explaining treatment before starting it.

  1. Examination & diagnosis

    Your teeth, gums and any symptoms are examined, with x-rays where needed, to find out exactly what is going on.

  2. A clear treatment plan

    Findings are explained plainly, and you are told which treatment is indicated for you — and what the alternatives are.

  3. Treatment in a sterile setup

    Care is delivered in modern operatories with a focus on sterilization and patient comfort.

  4. Aftercare & follow-up

    You leave with written-style aftercare guidance, and the clinic follows up — available any day of the week.
