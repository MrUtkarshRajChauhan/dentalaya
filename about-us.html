About us

Home for quality dental care

Adi's Dentalaya is a multispeciality dental and oral clinic in Rajajinagar — placed for easy access, equipped for advanced treatment, and run with an obsession for explaining care before delivering it.

Clinic profile

Conveniently placed, properly equipped

Adi's Dentalaya is conveniently placed in Rajajinagar with easy access, at the clinic's location on Ram Mandir Road in the 2nd block. Here, patients receive the best indicated, quality, advanced dental care — the phrase matters: treatment follows a diagnosis of what the tooth actually needs.

Services begin with dental diagnosis and stretch through fillings, root canal treatment and extractions to crowns/cap and bridge work — plus dental implants, smile designing, orthodontic corrections, periodontal surgeries and advanced dental treatment procedures.

How the practice thinks

Clear proposals before procedures

The clinic's own information pages tackle dental myths head-on — because fear and folklore delay care. Scaling will not loosen healthy teeth; upper-tooth removal will not damage the eyes; and children's milk teeth are treated because each one guards the space of the permanent tooth that follows.

After treatment, the clinic publishes its post-operative instructions on the website, so families can follow aftercare exactly as prescribed, any hour of the day.
