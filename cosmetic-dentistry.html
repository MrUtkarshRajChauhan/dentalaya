Cosmetic dentistry

Smile design, with honest advice

Teeth whitening, porcelain veneers and cosmetic bonding at Adi's Dentalaya — explained the way we'd explain it to a patient sitting in front of us: what each can do, who it suits, and what it cannot promise.

Brighten

Teeth whitening & bleaching

A bright white smile looks youthful and healthy — and professional whitening can deliver noticeable results in a short period. In-office treatment uses a stronger whitening agent with a barrier protecting your gum line, activated with light where required; at-home whitening uses custom trays worn daily for one to three weeks.

  • Common causes of discolouration: coffee, tea and red wine, smoking, tartar, tooth decay, injury and some medications taken during childhood
  • Healthy teeth and gums come first — gum disease, cavities and decay need attention before bleaching
  • Whitening agents do not change the colour of fillings, crowns, veneers, implants or dentures; results are not permanent and depend on your habits

Not recommended during pregnancy or for children — your dentist will confirm suitability after an examination.

Reshape

Dental porcelain veneers

Veneers are thin layers of ceramic bonded to the front of your teeth. Because they are stain resistant and natural looking, they can solve several aesthetic concerns at once — changing the position, shape, size and colour of teeth.

  • Often used for discolouration that whitening cannot fix, chipped or worn teeth, irregularly shaped teeth, gaps between teeth and mild misalignment
  • Usually completed in two visits: the enamel is reduced by a fraction of a millimetre, an impression goes to the lab, and the veneer is bonded about one to two weeks later
  • The enamel reduction is irreversible, and veneers eventually need replacement — typically around ten years — so the decision deserves a proper consultation

Repair

Cosmetic dental bonding

For chipped, fractured or discoloured teeth, bonding is a quick and affordable repair. Tooth-coloured composite resin is shaped and polished to match your surrounding teeth, then hardened with a high-intensity curing light — usually in a single visit.

  • Can also close small gaps between teeth, replace silver fillings, and protect roots exposed by receded gums
  • Painless, needs little or no enamel removal and very little of your tooth structure is lost
  • Composite is less durable than porcelain and can stain with coffee, tea, red wine or smoking — with good care it typically serves for several years before attention

How a cosmetic consultation runs

One conversation, three checkpoints

  1. Health check first

    Teeth and gums are examined; whitening and veneers require healthy foundations before any cosmetic work begins.

  2. Options compared honestly

    Whitening, bonding, veneers or crowns — you hear what each involves, how long it lasts and its limits, in plain language.

  3. Your goals, your budget

    You choose the treatment that fits what you want to achieve. Nothing irreversible starts until you have considered it carefully.
